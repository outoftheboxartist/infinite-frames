Privacy

Infinite Frames · last updated 20 November 2026

Infinite Frames is one shared, living artwork that anyone can add to. This page says what we keep and why.

What we keep

  • If you sign in with Google: your name, email address and profile photo, as Google provides them. Your name and photo appear next to what you add, so others can see who made it. Your email is not shown to anyone.
  • If you draw without signing in: an anonymous ID kept in your browser, so your work stays linked to you on that device.
  • What you add: curves, drawings, images you upload, chat messages, votes and saved places. These are public parts of the artwork.
  • A name you choose in the app, and small settings kept in your browser.

Where it is kept

Data is stored with Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage). The site is hosted by Vercel. Both process data only to run the service.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, show ads, or share your email. We don't use your data to train AI models.

Your choices

You can use Infinite Frames without signing in. You can sign out at any time. To ask for your account or your contributions to be removed, open an issue at github.com/outoftheboxartist/infinite-frames or ask in the app's chat; removal of published work may leave a note in the artwork's history that something was removed.

Changes

If this page changes, the date above changes with it.
